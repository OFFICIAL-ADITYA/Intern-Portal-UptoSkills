# UPTOSKILL INTERN PORTAL - OFFICIAL KNOWLEDGE BASE & SUPPORT GUIDE

## 1. About UptoSkills
UptoSkills is an experiential learning and career enablement platform providing specialized technical internships, hackathons, and industry mentorship. The Intern Portal is the centralized web system where candidates manage their profiles, request certificates, communicate with team leaders, and verify credentials.

## 2. Certificate Types & Eligibility
The portal issues several types of accredited certificates:
- **Internship Offer Letter**: Officially conformed upon selection and onboarding into the internship program.
- **Bonafide Certificate**: Issued to registered interns for university submission, academic credits, or college verification.
- **On-the-Job Training (OJT) Certificate**: Verifies applied technical training, project contributions, and workplace competencies.
- **Experience Letter**: Formal corporate letter detailing the intern's tenure, role, tasks performed, and accomplishments.
- **Certificate of Completion**: Conferred upon successfully fulfilling all internship requirements, milestones, and final evaluations.
- **Intern of the Month Certificate**: Prestigious recognition awarded to top-performing interns for exceptional performance.
- **League Winner Certificate**: Awarded to champions and winning teams in hackathons and project leagues.
- **Certificate of Achievement / Custom**: Customized honorary citations for special contributions.

## 3. How to Request a Certificate
1. Log in to your intern dashboard at `/login`.
2. On the **Overview** tab, click the **Request Certificate** button.
3. Select the desired certificate type from the dropdown.
4. Enter an optional reason or specific note (up to 500 characters).
5. Click **Submit Request**. Your request will appear in your **My Requests** table with status **Pending**.

## 4. Request Lifecycle & Tracking Statuses
- **Pending**: Request is newly submitted and waiting for review by your assigned Team Leader.
- **Processing**: The Team Leader has forwarded your request, and it is under review by the Admin team for draft generation.
- **Completed**: The Admin has approved and finalized your official certificate. You can immediately click **Download PDF** to download it.
- **Rejected**: The request was declined by the Team Leader or Admin. You can view the specific rejection reason in your requests list.
- **Revoked**: In rare administrative cases, an already issued certificate has been officially cancelled.

## 5. How to Download Your Certificate PDF
Once your request reaches the **Completed** status:
1. Go to your intern dashboard and scroll down to the **My Requests** section.
2. Find the row for your completed certificate.
3. In the Action column, click **Download PDF**.
4. The system will download your high-resolution official PDF certificate.

## 6. Certificate Verification & QR Codes
Every certificate generated by UptoSkills includes:
- A unique, scannable **QR code** on the certificate document.
- A 12-character alphanumeric **Verification ID** (`verifyId`).
- An official verification code (`verificationCode`).
Anyone (employers, universities, recruiters) can verify the certificate by:
- Scanning the QR code using any smartphone camera.
- Visiting `https://uptoskills.com/verify/<verifyId>` or `/verify/<verifyId>`.
The verification page shows real-time authenticity status, recipient name, domain, dates of tenure, and certificate ID.

## 7. Account Management, Passwords & Settings
- **First-Time Password Setup**: When you first receive your intern account, your initial temporary password/secret is your **Intern Code** (e.g., INT-2026-0042). Navigate to **Settings** on your dashboard to set a personal, permanent password.
- **Changing Password**: In dashboard Settings, enter your current password, your new password (minimum 6 characters), and confirm.
- **Updating Profile**: You can update your mobile number directly in the **Settings** panel. Other core details (Name, Domain, Intern Code) are managed by administration.
- **Forgot Password**: On the login page, click **Forgot Password?**, enter your registered email, and a secure reset link will be sent to your inbox.

## 8. Role Guidance & Contacts
- **Team Leader (TL)**: Evaluates weekly task submissions, monitors progress, and reviews initial certificate requests. Your assigned TL's email address is displayed on your intern dashboard profile card.
- **Admin**: Oversees template designs, custom drafts, final certificate issuance, retention policies, and platform audit logs.
- **Support**: For technical portal assistance, use this Assistant widget or contact info@uptoskills.com.

## 9. Dashboard Features & Mobile Navigation
- **Journey Timeline**: Displays official Start Date, connecting arrow, End Date, and an animated progress bar indicating overall tenure completion percentage.
- **Key Metric Stat Cards**: Shows Role, Domain, Days Remaining, and Completion rate at a glance.
- **Mobile Access**: The portal is fully mobile-responsive. On smartphones, a bottom dock provides fast navigation between Dashboard, Certificates, and Settings, and this Assistant widget opens as an easy-to-use bottom-sheet drawer.
